Use on Your Phone

Cadence is built as a Progressive Web App (PWA). Add it to your phone's Home Screen for instant one-tap tracking and late dose notifications:

iPhone (Safari)

Tap the Share icon at the bottom of Safari, scroll down, and tap "Add to Home Screen".

Android (Chrome)

Tap the ⋮ Menu in the top-right corner and select "Install App" or "Add to Home Screen".

Late Reminders & Audio Chimes

Tap "Enable Phone Push" on the main screen to allow system-level alerts whenever a scheduled dose becomes overdue.
